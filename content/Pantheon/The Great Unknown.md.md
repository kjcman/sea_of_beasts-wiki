**Domains:** All father  
**Titles:** Father of all things, the All-knowing, Giver of life

## Description: 

It is believed by many that there is a God of all gods, that all good gods serve Him and his perfect image for the world. However, No one knows His name, where He is, or how He came to be. There are a widespread organization of nomadic monks, clerics and scholars called [[The Acolytes of the Unknown]] that can be found on most major civilized islands. This organization is dedicated to the search for knowledge through exploration, meditation and reading. Their goal is to find out where the Great Unknown is, why he isn’t active in the world and how to bring him back. 

It is believed that The Great Unknown created the known world through hymns and shanties, for The Great Unknown loves music and song. Through the different shanties that he sang, facets of the world came to be. With every shanty came a different aspect of the world, the seas, the lands, the depths, the sky, the stars, the creatures, humanoids, and the lesser gods. The lesser gods recognized who gave them life and they sought to perfect their individual aspects of the Great Unknown’s world. 

### DM Notes: 

The evil [[Tākuroa]] tricked [[Hekallah]] into shaping a clever trap for The Great Unknown which put him into a deep slumber. [[Hekallah]], believing Tākuroa’s deception that it would please the All father to see him practice such a cunning display of his gifts, feels immensely burdened for being indirectly responsible for The All father’s absence from the world. Therefore She is one of the greatest fighters for The Great Unknown and His return, albeit in her own cunning, sometimes deceitful ways.
