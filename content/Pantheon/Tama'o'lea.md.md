**Pronunciation:** (Tah-mah-O-Lee-ah
**Domains:** Truth, Justice, Righteousness
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, The Voice of Truth

## Description

Tama’o’lea is revered as the unyielding guardian of honesty, clarity, and justice. He is both loved and feared, for while he rewards honesty with blessings of clarity and prosperity, he punishes liars with visions of their worst fears, forcing them to confront their inner deceit. Some myths say that his voice alone can shatter mountains and it is believed that Tama’o’lea will be the final judge before entering into the afterlife. His followers believe that truth is the foundation of all harmony, and only through living truthfully can one achieve be their best self.

### DM Notes

Followers of Tama'o'lea and Hekallah are often enemies or find each other at odds with each other. Both sets of followers believe the other to be the antithesis of the other, when really, Tama'o'lea and Hekallah are brother and sister. In fact, After being deceived by Tākuroa, Hekallah saught out his brother to right his this wrong and bring justice to the world. 

## Tenets

1. **Always speak the truth:**  Honesty must prevail, no matter the cost.
2. **Unveil the Shadows:** Seek and reveal hidden lies and illusions.
3. **Justice for the Wronged:** Use truth to bring balance and restore fairness to the world.
4. **The Inner Truth:** Embrace your shortcomings and accept who you are. Only then will you reach your full potential.
