**Pronunciation:** (Mah-nah-KEH-ah)
**Domains:** Power, Volcanoes, Resilience, Strength
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, Mother of Fury 

## Description

Manakéa is often depicted as a towering figure cloaked in flowing lava, her skin a blend of obsidian and molten gold. Her eyes burn with the intensity of a volcanic eruption, and her hair cascades like rivers of glowing magma. She wields a great staff or club shaped like a jagged shard of volcanic rock, known as Ka’uhi Nui (“The Great Flame”). Her sacred symbol is a jagged circle of obsidian with a molten core, representing both destruction and creation.

## Tenets

1. **Rise from Ashes:** Like land reborn from lava, embrace failure as an opportunity for renewal and greater strength.
2. **Strength Above All:** True power lies in resilience, not dominance. Forge your strength through trials and struggle.
3. **Fury with Purpose:** Destruction is not inherently evil; it is a path to creation. Use power responsibly, but never shy from unleashing it.
4. **Respect the Elements:** The earth, fire, and sea are sacred forces. To disrespect them is to invite ruin.
