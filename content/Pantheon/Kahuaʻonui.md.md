**Pronunciation:** (Kah-hoo-ah-oh-NOO-ee)
**Domains:** Artifice, Craft, Construction, Innovation 
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, The Forgewright, The Eternal Architect

## Description

Kahua is worshiped by inventors, blacksmiths, stonemasons, and engineers who seek inspiration and skill in their work. It is believed that Kahua crafted the first tools from volcanic stone. Depictions of Kahua often show a towering figure forged of bronze and steel, wielding a hammer in one hand and a compass in the other. His divine symbols include a gear encircling a hammer.

## Tenets

1. **Creation is Divine:** To build is to honor Kaelthar’s eternal ingenuity.
2. **Perfection Through Practice:** Strive for mastery in your craft, knowing that even flaws are lessons.
3. **Preserve and Innovate:** Respect ancient techniques but never shy from pushing boundaries
