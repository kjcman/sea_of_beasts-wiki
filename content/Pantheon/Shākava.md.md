**Pronunciation:** (Shau-Ka-vah)
Demon of Slavery and Subjugation
**Titles:** The Shackler of Souls

## Description

Shākava is said to have been born from the first betrayal among men—when one chief enslaved another instead of offering mercy. His body is woven from the cries of the oppressed, his eyes burn with the embers of lost rebellions, and his voice is the rustling of chains in the night.

Ancient seafarers whispered of Shākava appearing in times of conquest and war, feeding on the misery of captives. He does not simply bind the body but ensnares the soul, making his victims believe that submission is destiny. He thrives in the shadows of unjust rulers, lurking wherever power corrupts.

Shākava is described as a towering, shadowed figure with shifting, glowing tattoos across his body—each symbol representing a life he has shackled. His arms stretch unnaturally long, his fingers taper into hooks, and his mouth is filled with jagged teeth that never close, always whispering false promises. Some legends say his breath smells of seawater and blood, for he drags his victims into an ocean of servitude from which they can never escape.
