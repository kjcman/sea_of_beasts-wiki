**Pronunciation:** (Rah-Kah-tuh-LOH-ah)
Demon of Blood
**Titles:** Flesh Eater, Demon of Blood and Bone

## Description: 

Rakatu is a demon that slumbers beneath volcanic islands, awakening only when blood flows freely across the land. It is depicted with a gaping, circular maw full of spiraling teeth, surrounded by ever-weeping eyes. Wherever battles rage or sacrifices are made, Rakatu is near, drinking the spilt blood through cracks in the stone. Some say the red pools that appear after a battle are its eyes—watching, waiting, feeding.
