**Pronunciation:** (Mo-ah-nah-lee)
**Domains:** Sea, tides, waves, currents, storms
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, The Endless Tide, Mother of Waves

## Description

Moanali is depicted as a serene and regal figure whose form flows like the ocean itself. Her skin shimmers with the hues of turquoise and deep blue, her hair long and wavelike, adorned with seashells and glowing pearls. Her eyes reflect the boundless depths of the sea, glinting with wisdom and mystery. She carries Tāpuhi Moana (“Ocean’s Embrace”), a coral-staff that can calm even the fiercest storms or summon tidal waves. She is often symbolized with a spiral wave encircling a pearl which represents her endless movement and balance between calm seas and turbulent storms.

## Tenets

1. **Flow with the Current:** Adapt to life’s challenges as water shapes the land. Flexibility and persistence lead to triumph.
2. **Honor the Sea:** Protect the waters that sustain life, and never take more than is needed. Respect her creatures and her power.
3. **Protect the Vulnerable:** Like the sea and currents aids the bountiful life below, it is the strong who must look out for the weak. 
4. **Balance Above All:** Chaos and calm are both part of the sea’s nature. Embrace this duality and seek harmony in all things.**
