**Pronunciation:** (Heh-call-ah)
**Domains:** Trickery, Deception and Cleverness
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, The Weaver of Shadows and Smiles

## Description

A divine prankster, Hekallah delights in teaching mortals the art of illusion and misdirection, challenging them to think creatively in the face of adversity. Hekallah is often depicted as a shapeshifter, taking forms that blend beauty and danger—such as a playful dolphin that leads sailors astray, or a radiant flower that conceals a venomous creature. Followers of Hekallah believe that wit and cunning are as vital as strength and that laughter, even at one’s own expense, is the truest sign of humility. Hekallah’s symbol is a crescent moon and a fishhook, signifying guidance hidden in riddles.

### DM Notes

Hekallah is one of the greatest champions of the Unknown as she seeks to right her part in the Allfather’s disappearance from the world, though she doesn’t know how or where to even start.

Followers of Tama'o'lea and Hekallah are often enemies or find each other at odds with each other. Both sets of followers believe the other to be the antithesis of the other, when really, Tama'o'lea and Hekallah are brother and sister. In fact, After being deceived by Tākuroa, Hekallah saught out his brother to right his this wrong and bring justice to the world. 

## Tenets

1. **Outsmart the Strong:** Cunning triumphs where brute strength fails.  
2. **Find Humor in Chaos:** Embrace the unexpected and learn to laugh at adversity.
3. **Shapeshift Your Soul:** Adaptability and flexibility are keys to survival.
