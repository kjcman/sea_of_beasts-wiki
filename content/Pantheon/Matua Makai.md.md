**Pronunciation:** Ma-tu-ah Ma-kai
**Domains:** Death, Memory and Redemption
**Titles:** Keeper of Lost Names, She who Watches

## Description

Matua Makai rules over The  Cave of Night, a sacred cavern within The Reef - [[The Planes of Ta’onua#^63f105]]. Unlike the soul eaters within the abyss, she is not malevolent; she is a sacred guide who watches over the spirits of the dead and offers sanctuary to those worthy of redemption. Souls who redeem themselves are given a choice by She Who Watches, to be escorted by her to Te Māla [[The Planes of Ta’onua#^1ffea5]] or to serve her as a spirit guide for other lost souls. Her spirit guide servants take on the appearance of an animal of the sea, called [[Tipua]] who can guide the living. 

Matua Makai appears as a tall, graceful woman cloaked in midnight feathers and flowing kelp. Her face is partially obscured by a **ceremonial death mask**, carved from driftwood and inlaid with obsidian. Her many eyes (sometimes literal, sometimes symbolic) see past illusion, memory, and time. Her voice is a layered whisper of surf, breath, and ancestors speaking in harmony.

## Tenets

1. All Things Return to the Tide
	Grief is honored, but possession—of life, of others, of power—is a defiance of the cycle.
2. Name the Dead, That They May Be Known
	- Speak the names of the fallen in ritual and shanty.
    - Keep stories, carve legacies, and ensure no soul is left nameless or adrift.
3. Guide What Wanders, End What Refuses
	- Show mercy to lost spirits but release those who refuse the tide.
    - Banish those who cling to life out of greed, vengeance, or fear.
4. Honor the Balance Between Realms
	Spiritual crossings are sacred and dangerous—only the prepared may tread the border safely.
