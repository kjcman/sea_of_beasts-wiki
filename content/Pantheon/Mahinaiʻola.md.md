**Pronunciation:** MAH-hee-nie-oh-LAH)
**Domains:** Nature, Agriculture, Harvest, Renewal
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, The Blossom Queen 

## Description

Mahinai is revered as the gentle goddess of orchards, gardens, and the harvest. Farmers, gardeners, and caretakers of the land invoke her name to bless their crops and ensure fruitful seasons. She is often depicted as a serene figure with flowing hair resembling a cascade of vines and blossoms, her robes adorned with patterns of ripe fruit and blooming flowers. Her divine symbol is a wreath of blossoms encircling a golden sickle.

## Tenets

1. **Nurture the Land:** Care for the earth, for it gives life to all.  
2. **Celebrate Growth:** Cherish every seed planted, every blossom opened, and every fruit harvested.
3. **Honor the Cycle:** Embrace the seasons of growth, rest, and renewal.
