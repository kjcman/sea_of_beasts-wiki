**Pronunciation:** (Tau-kur-oa)
**Domains:** Chaos, Ocean Depths, Monsters, Destruction
**Titles:** The Abyssal Maw, Master of the depths, father of monsters and chaos

## Description

Tākuroa is the antithesis of good in the world and is Chaotic Evil Through and Through. He is the master of monsters and other abyssal creatures of the depths. He despises order. When the Great Unknown created the world, Tākuroa, who was originally created as a necessary force of chaos and destruction, quickly realized that he wanted more and schemed over centuries on how he was going to neutralize the Allfather and shape The Great Unknown’s world as he saw fit. 

While the The Great Unknown created and surrounds himself with lesser gods of great power who serve him in his great work, Tākuroa only surrounds himself with demons and demigods. Tākuroa is a jealous god and wishes for no one to acknowledge or adulate anyone except himself. Tākuroa enjoys proving his superiority over his demons by striking them down when they step out of line even in the slightest. 

Tākuroa is described as a being of immense size, his true form impossible for mortal minds to comprehend fully. He is often depicted with a writhing body of eels and tentacles, made of liquid shadow. Coral-like spines jut out from his body, glowing faintly with bioluminescence, while his eyes burn like molten suns. His voice is said to sound like the crushing of waves and the groaning of the ocean floor. His symbol is A spiraling vortex of black water, surrounded by jagged coral spikes all surrounding an eye glowing like a molten sun.

## Tenets


1. **Embrace the Abyss:** Seek the unknown and dive into the depths of chaos. Only by abandoning fear and order can one uncover true power.
2. **Shatter Order:** Reject the constructs of balance and control. Where order thrives, it is your duty to sow discord and upheaval.
3. **The Sea’s Wrath is Sacred:** Supernatural storms, tsunamis, and sea monsters are manifestations of Tākuroa’s will. Celebrate their destruction as sacred acts of his chaotic influence.
