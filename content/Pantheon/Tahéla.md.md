**Pronunciation:** Tah-Hae-lah
**Domains:** War, Peace, Courage
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, The Twofold Tide

## Description

Tahéla appears as a regal woman with skin like sun-warmed bronze and eyes that shift between tranquil blue and storm-gray. Her long hair flows like the ocean before a storm—dark at the roots, pale at the tips—and is adorned with coral, shells, and woven red fibers. One half of her body bears flowing red tatoo that glow like lava; the other half is inked in soft white spirals that pulse like moonlight through water. She carries a great club lined with shark teeth in one hand and a plumeria flower in the other, symbols of battle and harmony united.

Tahéla is the goddess of both **war and peace**, embodying the eternal rhythm between conflict and calm. She is the tide that destroys and the tide that renews. To her followers, she teaches that war and peace are not opposites, but phases in the same divine cycle — the breaking of the wave and the stillness that follows.

When sailors see the horizon streaked red at dawn, they say Tahéla has bathed her hands in war. When the sea glows silver under a full moon, they say she walks among mortals to bring reconciliation. Her temples are built on the shoreline — where neither land nor sea fully rules — reminding her faithful that balance is found only at the meeting of forces.

Tahéla is the daughter of [[Tākuroa]], a lineage she bears with sorrow. She rejects her father’s defiance and instead devotes herself to [[The Great Unknown]], striving to bring about His revival through the wars she wages against her father’s forces. Yet beneath her divine strength lies a quiet longing — Tahéla’s heart belongs to [[Tama’o’lea]], The voice of Truth that she loves from afar. She has never confessed her feelings, for she deems herself unworthy, burdened by the shadow of her parentage and the blood that binds her to [[Tākuroa]]’s legacy.

## Tenets

- **“Peace Is the Shore; War Is the Tide.”**: Both are sacred and necessary. One gives life; the other defends it. Seek peace first — but when it breaks, let your courage rise like the sea
- **“Harmony Is Forged in the Fire of Struggle.”**: Avoid conflict born of pride, but do not flee from righteous battle. Every hardship faced with honor strengthens the spirit and the world.
- **“Lay Down the Blade, Wash It Clean.”**: After every battle—whether of arms, words, or will—seek stillness. Make offerings to the sea, cleanse the weapon and the heart, and restore what was broken.
