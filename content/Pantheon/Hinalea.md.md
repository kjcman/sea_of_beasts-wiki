**Pronunciation:** (Hin-ah-Lee-ah)
**Domains:** Moon, Stars, Navigation, Tides
**Titles:** Servant of the Unknown, Celestial Guide of Ocean Roads, Mother of Stars

## Description

Hinalea is often envisioned as a radiant, silver-skinned goddess adorned in robes woven from mist and starlight. Her long, dark hair flows like liquid night, scattered with glowing shells and stars. She travels the sky each night in a great celestial canoe, navigating the cosmos and lighting the paths for sailors. Her voice is said to echo on the waves, carried in the wind, guiding voyagers through fog and fear. In legends, she gifted the first wayfinders with the knowledge of star maps and the secrets of ocean swells, teaching them to "read the sky as one reads a scroll." She is both gentle and vast, wise and distant. When she hides her face (new moon), it's a time of mystery and deep introspection; when she is full, it is said she watches over the world most closely along with her sister, Moanali, goddess of the sea.

## Tenets

1. Let the stars be your compass and your spirit be your sail: Trust in the signs of the sky and the instincts within. 
2. The moon waxes and wanes—so too must you ebb and flow: Embrace cycles of growth and rest, presence and absence, light and shadow.
3. Honor the ocean: Respect the sea as sacred. Never waste her bounty or ignore her moods.
